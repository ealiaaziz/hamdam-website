---
id: mfa-new-phone
title: New phone, and the authenticator code no longer arrives
symptoms:
  - new phone
  - authenticator
  - mfa not working
  - two factor
  - verification code
  - cannot approve sign in
  - lost my phone
clarifying:
  - Do you still have the old phone, even switched off in a drawer?
  - Are you being asked to approve a notification, or to type a six digit code?
---

Moving to a new phone is the most common reason approval stops working. The
authenticator app does not usually move across in a phone backup, so the new
phone has no record of the account even though everything else came over.

If you still have the old phone:

1. Keep it switched on and connected to wifi.
2. Sign in at https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info using the old
   phone to approve.
3. Add the new phone from that page, then remove the old one.

If the old phone is gone, wiped, or sold:

1. Stop and reply here. Adding a new device without an existing one almost
   always needs an administrator, and there is no self-service path that is
   also safe.
2. Tell us whether you have a work mobile number registered, because that
   sometimes gives a second route.

If your phone was lost or stolen rather than replaced, say that clearly in
your reply and we will treat it as urgent rather than routine, because the
account needs protecting before it needs restoring.
