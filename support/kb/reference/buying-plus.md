The free tier gives fifteen reflections a month, always, with no time limit
on it.

Hamdam Plus adds unlimited reflections, the reflection archive and insights
across your whole history, iCloud sync between your devices, Health body
signals, and the poet deep-dives beyond Hafez and Rumi. It is sold monthly or
yearly, with a seven day free trial for new subscribers.

Founding Companion is the one-time purchase: yours for life, shareable with
your family through Apple Family Sharing, up to six members.

Everything is bought through Apple. Apple sets the price, displays it at the
point of purchase, and takes the payment against the Apple ID. Never quote a
figure: it varies by region and Apple can change it.

What is deliberately not written down here is the sequence of taps inside the
app that opens the purchase screen. Nobody has verified it for the current
build, so do not describe it, do not reason it out from how apps like this
usually work, and do not offer the App Store "Purchased" list, which is the
restore flow for something already bought and not a way to buy anything.
Someone asking where to subscribe gets handed to a person who can open the
app and look.
