Reading and writing the calendar are separate opt-ins. Turning one on does
not turn on the other.

Reading: free and busy time only. Never event titles, descriptions,
attendees, or locations.

Writing: optional reflection blocks and cultural moment reminders. Everything
Hamdam creates goes into a dedicated calendar named "Hamdam" and is prefixed
"Hamdam:" so it is always distinguishable.

Hamdam identifies its own events by the calendar's identifier rather than by
matching title text. Renaming an event therefore does not put it beyond
Hamdam's reach, and does not pull one of the person's own events into it.
Hamdam never modifies an event it did not create.
