Hamdam is an iPhone app: a daily reflection companion built around classical
Persian poetry. Each day it offers a verse and a reflection, and it can take
account of signals you choose to share, such as sleep or weather, to shape the
tone of that reflection rather than to score anything about you.

The poets are Hafez, Rumi, Saadi, Khayyam and Parvin Etesami.

It is explicitly not medical, mental health, or wellness advice, and it is not
a substitute for professional care.
