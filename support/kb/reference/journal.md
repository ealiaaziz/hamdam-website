title: Journal entries
source: src/pages/terms.astro sections 6.1 and 6.2
---
People own their journal entries. Hamdam holds no licence to that content and
any entry can be deleted at any time.

Journals sync only through the person's own iCloud account, and only with
Hamdam Plus. Hamdam does not share journal content with anyone: there is no
public feed and no social features.

There is no Hamdam-side copy. If someone has lost entries and had no iCloud
sync enabled, there is nowhere to recover them from, and saying so honestly is
better than raising hope. That conversation goes to a person.
