The core library is 235 verses, built into the app. It needs no network
connection to read. A blank or missing verse is therefore not an internet
problem, and telling someone to check their connection is wrong.

Hamdam Plus subscribers additionally receive a growing pool of verses stored
in a CloudKit public database and fetched as it grows. Free users never fetch
that pool.

Verses are public domain, from Hafez, Rumi, Saadi, Khayyam and Parvin
Etesami, sourced from the Ganjoor.net archive with attribution. No data is
exchanged with Ganjoor.net while the app runs. Hamdam's curation, English
translations and cultural context are its own copyright.

Persian poetry uses wine, love and mystical imagery as literary and spiritual
metaphor. These are historical conventions of the form, not endorsements of
anything.
