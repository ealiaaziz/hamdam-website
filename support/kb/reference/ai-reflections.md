---
id: ai-reflections
title: How reflections are generated
source: src/pages/privacy.astro section 1.7
---
On supported hardware, iPhone 15 Pro and later running iOS 26 or newer,
reflections are generated on the device using Apple's Foundation Models. No
text and no personal data leaves the device for this.

On unsupported devices Hamdam falls back to a static, pre-written library of
content.

So "why are my reflections different from my friend's" or "why do these feel
less personal" usually comes down to device hardware, and that is worth
checking before anything else.
