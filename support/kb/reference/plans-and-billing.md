There is a free tier. Paid plans are Monthly, Yearly, and a one-time Lifetime
purchase.

- Pricing is set and shown by Apple at the point of purchase. Never quote a
  price: it varies by region and Apple can change it. Point people at the App
  Store listing.
- Monthly and Yearly include a 7 day free trial for new subscribers. The trial
  becomes a paid subscription unless it is cancelled before it ends.
- Lifetime supports Apple Family Sharing for up to six members. Monthly and
  Yearly are single user.
- Monthly and Yearly auto-renew at the then-current price, charged to the
  Apple ID, until cancelled.
- Cancelling: Settings, then their Apple ID at the top, then Subscriptions.
  Cancelling stops the next renewal and access continues to the end of the
  period already paid for.
- There is no refund for the unused part of a period, subject to Apple's own
  refund policy. Refunds are Apple's decision, not Hamdam's, and the desk
  cannot issue one. Requests go through Apple's "Report a Problem" page.
