title: Roots, cultural moments, and poet biographies
source: src/pages/privacy.astro section 5
---
The Roots calendar shows public holidays for where the person is. Those dates
come from Nager.Date (date.nager.at), which is the only service Hamdam
contacts that Apple does not operate. The device sends a country code and a
year, and nothing else.

Poet biographies and cultural symbol descriptions come from the Wikipedia REST
API, and portraits from upload.wikimedia.org. The device sends only the title
of the page being looked up. Nothing about the person, their reflections or
their health is included.
