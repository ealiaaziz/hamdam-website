title: The Discover tab
source: src/pages/privacy.astro section 1.6
---
Discover refreshes once a day with books and podcasts from Apple's public
catalogue, through the iTunes Search API.

Those requests go from the device straight to Apple. They never pass through
a Hamdam server, and no identifier tied to the person is sent with them.

Because it refreshes daily, "the recommendations have not changed" is
expected within a single day.
