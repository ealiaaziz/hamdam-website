All of this is opt-in and stays on the device.

With permission Hamdam reads resting heart rate, heart rate variability,
sleep analysis, blood oxygen, step count, exercise minutes, and active energy.
Menstrual flow is read only if cycle awareness is separately enabled, and
cycle awareness is a distinct opt-in from everything else here.

With permission Hamdam writes State of Mind entries to Apple Health when mood
logging is turned on.

These signals shape the tone of a reflection. They are not scored, charted, or
displayed back. Cycle data in particular is never shown anywhere in the
Hamdam interface.

Permissions are changed in iPhone Settings, under Privacy and Security, then
Health, then Hamdam.

Observations about sleep, activity or cycle are contextual, not medical
assessments. If someone is asking a health question rather than an app
question, that is for a qualified healthcare provider, and the ticket goes to
a person.
