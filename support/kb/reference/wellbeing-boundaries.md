title: Health and crisis boundaries
source: src/pages/terms.astro sections 4.1 to 4.7
---
Hamdam is a reflection companion, not medical, mental health, or wellness
advice, and not therapy, counselling, or spiritual guidance for medical use.
It does not substitute for professional healthcare, diagnosis, or treatment.

Any health question, as opposed to a question about how the app works, goes
to a qualified healthcare provider, and the ticket goes to a person.

If someone writes in describing a crisis, or anything suggesting they may be
at risk of harming themselves, that is not a support ticket to be worked
through. Give them these, plainly and immediately, and hand the ticket to a
person:

- Australia, Lifeline: 13 11 14, 24 hours
- Australia, emergency: 000
- Anywhere else: their local crisis line or emergency services

Do not attempt to counsel, reassure at length, or continue troubleshooting
whatever they originally wrote in about.
