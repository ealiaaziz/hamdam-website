There is an Apple Watch app, and it comes with Hamdam. It is not a separate
purchase and it is not part of Hamdam Plus. Nothing on the watch is behind the
subscription.

It needs watchOS 26.5 or later. That is the same requirement the App Store
listing states under Compatibility.

The watch app is a companion to the phone rather than a standalone one. It is
installed from the Watch app on iPhone, under My Watch, by finding Hamdam in
the list and turning on Show App on Apple Watch. If someone cannot find it
there, the usual causes are the phone app not being installed, the watch not
being paired to that phone, or the watch still syncing after a fresh pair.

Complications are available, so the verse of the day can sit on a watch face.
Those are added by editing the face on the watch or in the Watch app on iPhone,
the same way any complication is added.

Language follows the phone. Hamdam shows one language at a time rather than
both together, and the watch obeys the same setting as everything else.

Two things worth knowing before promising anything:

The watch reads what the phone has already worked out. If the phone has not
been opened in a while, or the two have not been near each other, the watch can
be showing an older day. Opening Hamdam on the phone and waiting a moment is
the fix, and it is usually the whole answer to "my watch is showing yesterday".

If a requester asks about sending a movement session from the Garden to the
watch, that is not something to confirm. Take the ticket to a person rather
than describing behaviour, because the answer depends on the app version they
are running.
