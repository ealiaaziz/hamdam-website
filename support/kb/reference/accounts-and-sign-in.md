---
id: accounts-and-sign-in
title: There are no accounts and no sign in
source: src/pages/privacy.astro section 2
---
Hamdam has no user accounts. There is no sign in, no password, no username,
and no email address collected when you start using it. Nothing to be locked
out of, and nothing to reset.

This matters when someone writes in about being unable to log in. They are
almost certainly describing one of:

- An Apple ID problem, which is Apple's, not Hamdam's, and is handled in
  iPhone Settings under their name.
- A purchase that has not appeared on a new device, which is a restore through
  the App Store rather than a sign in.
- iCloud sync not working, which needs a Hamdam Plus subscription and the
  device to be signed in to iCloud.

Ask which of those it is rather than assuming, and never walk someone through
signing in to Hamdam, because there is nowhere for them to do it.
