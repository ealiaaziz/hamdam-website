id: weather-and-location
title: Weather and location
source: src/pages/privacy.astro section 1.3
---
Location is used only when weather or cultural moment features are enabled.
Weather comes through Apple's WeatherKit and is used to theme the day's
reflection.

Hamdam does not store location and does not share it with anyone.

If weather looks wrong or missing, the usual cause is location permission
being off for Hamdam in iPhone Settings, under Privacy and Security, then
Location Services.
