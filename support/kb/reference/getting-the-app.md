Hamdam is an iPhone app, on the Apple App Store. There is no Android
version, no web version, and no desktop version.

Direct link: https://apps.apple.com/au/app/id6784461990

Or search the App Store for "Hamdam".

It is free to start and stays free: fifteen reflections every month, with no
time limit and no card needed. Hamdam Plus and the one-time Founding
Companion purchase are optional, bought through Apple from inside the app.

Someone asking where to get it, or how to download it, wants the link above.
That is a question the desk can answer completely, so answer it rather than
handing it over.
