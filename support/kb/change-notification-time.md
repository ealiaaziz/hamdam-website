---
id: change-notification-time
title: Change the daily reminder time
symptoms:
  - notification time
  - reminder time
  - change reminder
  - daily reminder
  - notification too early
  - notification too late
  - turn off notifications
clarifying:
  - Do you want to move the reminder, or turn it off entirely?
---

1. Open Hamdam and go to Settings.
2. Tap Daily reminder.
3. Set the time you would like, or switch the reminder off.

If reminders are not arriving at all, check iPhone Settings, then
Notifications, then Hamdam, and confirm Allow Notifications is on.
