Hamdam has no account and no sign in, so there is nothing to be locked out
of. What this usually is instead:

1. Check you are signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with. On iPhone,
   open Settings and look at the name at the top.
2. Restore the purchase from the App Store: open the App Store, tap your
   picture, then Purchased, and find Hamdam.
3. If it was a Lifetime purchase shared through Family Sharing, confirm the
   organiser still has sharing switched on for purchases.
4. Give it a minute after restoring and reopen Hamdam.

If it still does not appear, reply and tell us whether this is a new device,
and a person will pick it up.
