# Reel and card CTA

## The problem this solves

On 13 August a reel reached 1,063 viewers and produced 4 profile visits and
0 follows. The account has never gained a follower from a post. The CTA at
the time said "follow" and gave the handle - an instruction with no reason
attached.

Average watch time is 5.6 seconds on a 14 second reel. Page 5, where the
fuller pitch lives, begins at 11.5 seconds. Most viewers never reach it.
So the reason to follow has to be on screen from the first second.

## What is on screen now

Persistent strip, every page, from frame one:

    Follow  -  a verse every day   (Persian, with English beneath)

The ask and the reason in one line each. "A verse a day" is what a stranger
is actually deciding about; a handle is not. Instagram already shows the
handle at the top of every reel, so repeating it wasted the space.

Page 5, for the minority who reach it:

    Monday - Wednesday - Friday   (Persian, with English beneath)

A schedule is a promise about tomorrow, which is what creates the
anticipation a follow depends on. It is also always true, unlike naming
the next poet - poet rotation and the Rumi tripwire can both change which
concept runs next, and a broken promise is worse than a vague one.

## Rules for anyone editing this

- The ask lives in the strip, not on the last page. Do not move it back.
- Never promise a specific poet or verse for the next post.
- Keep both languages. 71% of the audience is 18-34 diaspora; Persian
  carries the meaning, English carries the reach.
- The send prompt on page 5 names one specific person ("someone who hasn't
  had a moment to sit"), never a category ("a friend"). A reader who
  pictures one face acts; a reader who pictures nobody scrolls.
