## Draft
Hamdam is a daily Persian poetry companion for iPhone, made in Brisbane. Each day it offers one verse from five classical poets: Hafez, Rumi, Saadi, Khayyam and Parvin Etesami, together with a short reflection chosen by how your day feels on a simple mood slider. There is a private journal for your own thoughts, favourites to keep verses close, and a streak that forgives a missed day. Verses are sourced from the Ganjoor corpus and credited. Hamdam is available now on the Australian App Store. If Persian poetry has a place in your day, come and find a quiet moment with it.
