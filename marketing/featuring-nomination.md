## What to nominate

**Nomination type:** app update, tied to version 1.3, released 2026-08-27.

Nominate the update rather than the app. A first release competes against
every new app that month; an update competes on whether the change is
interesting, and 1.3 is the more interesting story: a discovery tab that became
a garden, and a listing that went from two storefronts to ten.

Apple asks for the nomination roughly four to six weeks ahead of when you would
want the placement. 1.3 shipped 2026-08-27, so this is timely now and stops
being timely in October.

## Timing hook

Two dates make this app editorially obvious, and both are worth naming:

- **Mehregan**, early October, the autumn festival.
- **Yalda**, 21 December, the longest night. Iranians stay up reading Hafez
  until dawn. It is the single night of the year when a Hafez app is the most
  natural thing in the world, and it is the strongest hook this product will
  ever have.

Yalda is the one to build the nomination around if the timing allows: nominate
in October for a December placement.

## What is distinctive, in order

1. **The verses are the real thing.** 500 poems from Hafez, Rumi, Saadi,
   Khayyam and Parvin Etesami, sourced from Ganjoor and credited. Not a quote
   generator.
2. **Parvin Etesami is in the five.** A twentieth century Iranian woman writing
   in classical form, usually left out of the canonical four. Including her is
   a deliberate editorial position, not a rounding up.
3. **It adapts to who is reading.** Someone with no Persian connection gets
   genuinely different content, not a translated shell.
4. **It asks how your day feels first.** A mood chooses the verse. The poem
   answers the reader rather than the calendar.
5. **Nothing leaves the device that does not have to.** No accounts, no email,
   no sign-in. The journal is local.
6. **Ten storefronts, built by one person in Brisbane.** Australian
   trade mark 2674427.

## Honest expectation

Featuring is a lottery and this app is small: 64 first-time downloads and one
paying subscriber as of 2026-08-26. The nomination costs an hour and nothing
else, which is why it is worth filing, but it is not a plan. The Yalda angle is
the one thing here that a human editor might actually bite on, because it is a
real cultural moment with a date, and this app is the obvious thing to feature
on it.
