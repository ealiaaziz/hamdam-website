---
id: printer-not-printing
title: Printer is not printing
symptoms:
  - printer not working
  - cannot print
  - printer offline
  - print job stuck
  - nothing comes out
clarifying:
  - Does the printer show anything on its own screen, such as an error or a paper jam?
  - Is it only your computer, or is nobody in the office able to print?
---

Whether it is only you decides where to look, so establish that first if you
can.

1. Check the printer itself for a light, a message on its panel, an open tray
   or a jam. A surprising share of print faults are visible from where the
   printer is standing.
2. On your computer, open the printer queue and clear any documents sitting in
   it. One failed job can hold up everything queued behind it.
3. If the printer shows as offline, switch it off at the wall, wait thirty
   seconds, and switch it back on. Give it a minute to come back before
   trying again.
4. Try printing a test page. If the test page works and your document does
   not, the problem is that document or that application, which is a different
   fix.

If nobody in the office can print, say so when you reply, and do not restart
the printer repeatedly first. That points at the network or the print queue
rather than your machine, and knowing it started for everyone at once is
useful information we lose once things have been power cycled.
