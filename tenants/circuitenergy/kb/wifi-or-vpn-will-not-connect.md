id: wifi-or-vpn-will-not-connect
title: No internet, wifi will not connect, or the VPN drops
symptoms:
  - no internet
  - wifi not working
  - cannot connect
  - vpn not connecting
  - vpn keeps dropping
  - network down
clarifying:
  - Can you reach any website at all, or is it only certain things that fail?
  - Are you in the office or working remotely?
---

Work out how far the connection gets before it fails, because that is what
separates a device problem from an outage.

1. Try loading a well known website. If nothing loads at all, it is the
   connection. If some things load and work systems do not, the connection is
   fine and something else is failing.
2. Turn wifi off and on again on the device. On a laptop, forget the network
   and rejoin it if it keeps refusing.
3. Check another device on the same wifi. If that also fails, the problem is
   the connection in that building rather than your machine.
4. For a VPN that connects and then drops, note roughly how long it stays up
   before dropping. A few seconds and a few minutes point at different
   things, and it is the first thing we will ask.

If the whole office is offline, restarting your own laptop will not help.
Reply and say when it started and whether anything changed just before, such
as a power cut or new equipment being plugged in.
